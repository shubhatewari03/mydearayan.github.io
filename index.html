And then somehow everything after that just kept unfolding.

But I do know that right now, in this little chapter of our lives, I am incredibly grateful that you're here. I'll remember the stupid arguments. I'll remember the late conversations. I'll remember the feeling of slowly realizing that “oh” “Oh, this person is actually becoming really important to me.”

The girl who will always have something random to tell you. The girl who thinks you're ridiculously special even when you're being absolutely unbearable.
